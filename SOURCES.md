## 3. Ukraine drones halve Russia's oil refining (Ukraine / Energy)
- Ukrainian drone attacks have cut Russian crude refining by 45% since
  late October, from more than 700,000 tonnes a day to about 400,000
  tonnes a day (around 3 million barrels a day). 15 of Russia's 35 large
  refineries are now disrupted or shut, up from 9 in mid-week, including
  plants in Ryazan, Yaroslavl and Kirishi.
- https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/drone-attacks-nearly-halve-russias-november-oil-refining-sources-say-2026-11-18/

## 4. Voyager 1 reaches one light-day away (Space / NASA)
- On 18 November 2026 at 10:16 UTC, Voyager 1 reached 25.9 billion km
  from Earth — exactly one light-day — becoming the first human-made
  object to reach that distance, almost 50 years after its launch on
  5 September 1977. A radio signal now takes 24 hours one way, so a
  question and its answer take two full days.
- https://www.sciencealert.com/its-official-voyager-1-is-the-first-spacecraft-to-reach-one-light-day-from-earth
- https://www.sciencealert.com/voyager-1-has-traveled-through-space-at-38000-mph-since-1977-and-this-wednesday-will-finally-reach-1-light-day

## 5. Peltola wins Alaska, Democrats take the Senate (United States / Politics)
- The Associated Press called the final Senate race of the 2026 midterms
  on 18 November (UTC): Democrat Mary Peltola defeated two-term Republican
  incumbent Dan Sullivan in Alaska, 50.1% (144,302 votes) to 45.8%
  (131,844), giving Democrats a 56–44 Senate majority — a nine-seat flip,
  the largest since 1958. Peltola, who is Yup'ik, is the first Alaska
  Native elected to the U.S. Senate. Results remain unofficial until
  certification, aimed for 25 November.

## 6. UK inflation hits a one-year high (United Kingdom / Economy)
- UK consumer price inflation rose to 3.6% in October 2026, from 3.5% in
  September — its highest in a year and further above the Bank of
  England's 2% target — in ONS data released on Wednesday 18 November.
  Fuel drove the rise: petrol up 5.2 pence a litre to 174.7p, and diesel
  at a record 199.5p a litre, the highest since ONS records began in 1996.
  The Bank of England warned inflation could approach 5% in 2027.
